\taskname{Definition / parametric type}{def-parametric-type}
Define signed \texttt{fx<W,F>} for $2\leq W\leq32$, $0\leq F<W$.
Use it in native signatures; preserve $W,F$ through construction and print/parse.
\boundary{$W=F=8$: reject with \texttt{invalid-type-parameter}.} &
\taskpair{type-input}{Width 8 and fraction 3.} &
\taskpair{type-output}{Round trip preserves both parameters. Width denotes
logical bits, not ABI size.} \\[4pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Definition / quantized operation}{def-quantized-op}
Define \texttt{qadd} on equal-shape i8 tensors, with finite positive scales and
signed-i32 zero points. Preserve operand shape and element type.
\boundary{\texttt{2x3xi8} plus \texttt{3x2xi8}: \texttt{shape-mismatch}.} &
\taskpair{qadd-input}{$x$: scale 0.5, zero 0.\par $y$: scale 0.25, zero $-3$.} &
\taskpair{qadd-output}{Preserve shape, scales, and zero points through
print/parse.} \\[4pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Analysis / shape broadcasting}{ana-broadcast-shape}
Broadcast nonnegative extents by trailing dimension. Pad the shorter shape
with ones. Each pair must agree or contain one; a one selects the other extent,
including zero.
\boundary{\texttt{[2,3]} and \texttt{[4,3]}: conflict at axis 1 from the end.} &
\taskpair{broadcast-input}{Rank-3 and rank-1 operands.} &
\taskpair{broadcast-output}{Legal. Also, \texttt{[2,0]} with \texttt{[1]}
gives \texttt{[2,0]}.} \\[4pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Analysis / numeric range}{ana-numeric-range}
Propagate closed finite intervals through add, multiply, ReLU, and clamp.
Multiplication considers all four endpoint products.
\boundary{ReLU on \texttt{[2,-1]}: \texttt{invalid-interval}.} &
\taskpair{range-input}{Multiply intervals spanning zero.} &
\taskpair{range-output}{Take the minimum and maximum of all four products.} \\[4pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Rewrite / neutral element}{rew-add-zero}
Replace integer add-by-zero when shape and type are preserved. Floating
positive zero additionally requires NSZ; retain negative zero. Erase a matched
constant only if it has no remaining uses.
\boundary{Adding \texttt{0.001} retains the operation.} &
\taskpair{zero-input}{Positive zero; NSZ enabled; shape \texttt{[4]}.} &
\taskpair{zero-output}{Redirect every result user to \texttt{x}; erase the
matched add and any now-unused constant.} \\[7pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Rewrite / redundant cast}{rew-redundant-cast}
Remove identity casts. Cancel round trips only for lossless signed-integer
widening or finite f32-to-f64 widening. Retain narrowing, float--integer,
and shape-changing casts.
\boundary{\texttt{i16 -> i8 -> i16}: keep both casts.} &
\taskpair{cast-input}{The input and result have identical type and shape.} &
\taskpair{cast-output}{Identity cast removed. Lossless \texttt{i8 -> i16 -> i8}
also redirects users to the original value.} \\[7pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Conversion / GELU expansion}{con-gelu-expand}
Replace native SSA \texttt{gelu} with $0.5x(1+\operatorname{erf}(x/\sqrt2))$
using declared primitive targets. Preserve shape, floating element type,
and every output user.
\boundary{Integer input: \texttt{unsupported-element-type}; no output IR.} &
\taskpair{gelu-input}{A single GELU on f32\texttt{[5]}.} &
\taskpair{gelu-output}{Final multiply: $0.5x(1+\operatorname{erf}(x/\sqrt2))$.
Constants appear in consuming ops; no GELU remains.} \\[4pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Conversion / quantized addition}{con-quant-expand}
Expand i8 \texttt{qadd} into two dequantizations, f32 add, and quantization
with RNE and i8 saturation. Preserve shape and result users.
\boundary{Negative scale: \texttt{invalid-scale}; no output IR.} &
\taskpair{quant-input}{\texttt{[-3,0,5,120]} plus \texttt{[2,1,-8,120]}.
All scales 0.5; zero points 0.} &
\taskpair{quant-output}{Result: \texttt{[-1,1,-3,127]}.
The last lane saturates from 240 to 127.} \\[7pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Emission / graph manifest}{emit-graph-manifest}
Emit deterministic schema-v1 JSON. Number nodes and values in stable order;
preserve repeated operands, output order, types, and sorted user attributes.
Omit declarations and source names.
\boundary{Repeated emission must be byte-identical.} &
\taskpair{manifest-input}{All values have type f32\texttt{[2,3]}. The input
receives identifier \texttt{v0}.} &
\taskpair{manifest-output}{Schema 1; output \texttt{v3}. Node \texttt{n0}
stores \texttt{value=1.25}; \texttt{n1} reads \texttt{[v0,v1]};
\texttt{n2} reads \texttt{[v2]}.} \\[4pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Emission / executable wrapper}{emit-kernel-wrapper}
Emit a complete C99 translation unit exporting
\texttt{task\_kernel(input,output,count)} for ReLU or $2x+1$.
Support exact in-place execution; preserve guards and nonaliased inputs.
\boundary{Zero count with null pointers: no memory access.} &
\taskpair{wrapper-input}{Negative, signed-zero, and positive input lanes.} &
\taskpair{wrapper-output}{Run the compiled artifact; both nonpositive lanes
produce positive zero.} \\[4pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Vertical / packed low-bit arithmetic}{vert-int4}
Add signed qint4; saturate to $[-8,7]$ and pack two's-complement nibbles
low-first. Zero the unused high nibble for odd counts. Reject out-of-range
literals before addition.
\boundary{Literal 8: \texttt{literal-out-of-range}.} &
\taskpair{int4-input}{Five lanes, including positive overflow.} &
\taskpair{int4-output}{Sums 9 and 14 saturate to 7. Hex bytes are
\texttt{09 7e 07}, with zero high padding.} \\[4pt]
\midrule

\taskname{Vertical / quantized fused operation}{vert-fused-op}
Define NHWC/HWIO i8 \texttt{fused\_qconv\_relu} with valid unit-stride
cross-correlation, i32 bias, RNE requantization, i8 saturation, and real-domain
ReLU. Fuse single-use chains only.
\boundary{Shared convolution output: no fusion.} &
\taskpair{fused-input}{Unit kernel: \texttt{x=[1,-2]}, \texttt{w=[2,-3]},
bias 1. Scales: accumulator 0.25, output 0.5; output zero point 0.} &
\taskpair{fused-output}{Accumulator $1(2)+(-2)(-3)+1=9$.
One fused node; output \texttt{[4]}.} \\[4pt]
